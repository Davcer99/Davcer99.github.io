# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro, static output (no SSR adapter), deployed to a static host (Netlify, Vercel or Pages). GSAP for motion. Isometric SVG illustrations produced with the anatomy-asset skill.

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers screening candidates for full-stack and AI roles. They arrive from LinkedIn, a CV or a direct link, scan quickly, and decide whether to reach out. Some read Spanish, some English.

## Product Purpose

Personal landing page for David Balaguer Cervera. It exists so a recruiter understands, within seconds, what kind of engineer he is and contacts him by email. Success means a recruiter sends an email or opens his LinkedIn.

## Positioning

A full-stack developer who integrates AI into real products: complete systems (frontend, backend, data) with AI and process automation built in as working features, not demos.

## Operating Context

- Visitors are evaluating a candidate, often alongside a CV or LinkedIn profile.
- Fully remote availability.
- Content in two languages: Spanish and English, equivalent in substance.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Primary action: email via `mailto:davidbalaguercervera@gmail.com`.
- Secondary action: LinkedIn profile, https://www.linkedin.com/in/david-balaguer-cervera/
- No form backend, no analytics decided.
- Undecided: GitHub profile, CV download, years of experience, named projects or employers.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: David Balaguer Cervera.
- Role line (user's words): "full stack developer especializado en ia".
- Must not read as a generic developer-portfolio template (dark + neon + terminal), as over-animated, as corporate and personality-free, or as AI hype (magic gradients, glow, vague promises).

## Evidence on Hand

None yet: no testimonials, clients, metrics, employers or project write-ups. Future work must not fabricate any of these. Illustrative material may be authored but must be labelled as an example wherever a visitor could take it as a real project or claim.

## Product Principles

1. Show how he works, do not claim it: concrete descriptions of what he builds beat adjectives.
2. AI as engineering, not magic: describe AI work in terms of systems, integrations and outcomes.
3. A recruiter's time is short: the role, the differentiator and the contact route are readable without scrolling.
4. Parity between languages: neither locale is a second-class translation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA as the baseline; motion must respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
