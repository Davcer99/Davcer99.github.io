## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is an assembly manual: David's profile ships as a kit whose parts (A backend, B frontend, C AI module, D automation) are drawn exploded and assembled in numbered steps. It refuses the split hero over a row of equal skill cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white paper #f6f7f8, black line art #121314 at 2.5px strokes, side faces #e4e6ea, rules #c9ccd1. Process blue #2d5bff is reserved for the AI module and the primary action.
